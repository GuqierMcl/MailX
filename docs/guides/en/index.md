This guide walks you through installing MailX, adding your mailboxes and getting the most out of everyday email. Not installed yet? Pick your system on the [download page](/en/download/).

## What is MailX?

MailX is a free, open-source desktop email app for macOS, Windows and Linux — with no ads and no MailX account required.

- **All your mailboxes in one inbox**: bring your work, personal and school accounts together instead of juggling multiple apps and browser tabs.
- **Your mail stays on your computer**: MailX has no mail server of its own. Your messages and passwords stay on your device, and downloaded mail remains readable offline.
- **AI and translation only when you want them**: translate foreign-language mail in one click, or connect the AI provider or local model you choose to summarize messages and help you write. Everything else works normally without AI.

## Start here

- [Install and update](/en/docs/install/): install MailX on macOS, Windows or Linux, and keep it up to date.
- [Add an account](/en/docs/add-account/): connect your mailboxes, including how to get an app password or authorization code.
- [Reading and organizing](/en/docs/reading/): explore Now, the unified inbox, date grouping, one-click verification codes and search.
- [Writing and sending](/en/docs/writing/): compose mail, add attachments, use local drafts, undo send and check the outbox.
- [Translate emails](/en/docs/translation/): translate foreign-language mail in place while keeping its layout.
- [Using the AI assistant](/en/docs/ai/): connect an AI provider or local model to ask about mail, summarize messages and help you write.
- [Privacy](/en/docs/privacy/): see what MailX keeps on your computer and when it connects to the network.
