MailX brings all your mailboxes into one inbox, keeps your mail on your own computer, and lets the AI you choose help you read and write.

Not installed yet? Pick your system on the [download page](https://mailxapp.com/en/download/).

The full guide is being rewritten. Until it is ready, see [Getting Started](https://github.com/GuqierMcl/MailX/blob/main/docs/guides/README.md) on GitHub for accounts, writing, translation, AI and more.

## Need help?

Tell us about a problem or an idea on [GitHub](https://github.com/GuqierMcl/MailX/issues). Please leave out passwords, real mail content and email addresses.
