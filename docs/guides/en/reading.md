Once your mailboxes are added, MailX syncs your recent mail and brings everything together in one clean workspace.

## Start with Now

**Now**, at the top of the sidebar, gives you a quick overview of your day: anything that needs attention, local drafts, verification codes from the last 15 minutes, new mail and recent activity. Everything on this page is worked out on your computer and never sent anywhere.

- Click any item to jump straight to that email or draft; hover over an unread row to star, mark read or archive in place, or use the per-account unread pills to jump directly into an account's inbox.
- Click **···** at the right of a section's title to **Hide for today** (it comes back automatically when there is something new) or **Don't show again**.
- Under **Settings → Now**, choose or preview the backdrop's ambient scene (automatic by time of day or any of the four fine-line scenes), turn hidden sections back on, or switch the order from Automatic to Fixed and drag sections into the order you prefer.
- Prefer to open straight into your inbox? Change it under **Settings → General → Open at startup**.

## Unified inbox and account folders

- The top of the sidebar brings together **Inbox**, **Starred**, **Drafts** and **Sent** from all your accounts, with Archive, Junk and Trash under **More**.
- Each mailbox is listed below; expand one to browse all of its folders on the server.
- Want more room to read? Click the sidebar toggle in the title bar or press `Cmd/Ctrl + B` to collapse it into a narrow column of icons.
- Each folder's first sync brings in the most recent **30 days** of mail (up to 1,000 messages). Messages you open are saved on your computer so you can read them offline later.

## Date grouping, layout and conversations

- **Group by date**: message lists and search results group mail by local date by default into **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week**, **Last week** and **Earlier** (Today and Yesterday take priority). Under **Settings → Appearance → Message list**, change **First day of the week** or turn off **Group by date** for a flat list.
- **Split view or single pane**: **Split view** (default) shows the list and message side by side; drag the divider to resize. Switch to **Single pane** under **Settings → Appearance → Message list** for focused reading, and press `Esc` to return to the list.
- **Conversations**: back-and-forth replies appear together as one thread, with your open message expanded and earlier or newer messages stacked as compact rows. Switch between **Conversation** and **Single** at the top of the reader anytime.
- **Folded quotes**: repeated quoted text at the bottom of a reply folds behind a **Show quoted text** button so new writing stands out. Replying or forwarding still includes the full original.

## Organizing and bulk actions

Hover over a message (or right-click it) for quick actions:

- **Star / Mark read or unread**: syncs back to your mail server.
- **Archive**: clears the message from your inbox; find it later under **More → Archive**.
- **Delete**: gives you a brief chance to undo, then moves the message to Trash on the server. Deleting from Trash asks to confirm and removes it permanently.
- **View or group by contact**: right-click a message and choose **Show mail from this contact** to see all mail from them, or **Group mail from this contact** to fold their messages into one row in your inbox (manage grouped contacts under **Settings → Contacts**).
- **Bulk actions**: click **Select** above the list, or `Cmd/Ctrl`-click / `Shift`-click to pick up to 500 messages at once, then mark read, star, archive or delete them together.

:::note
**Archive** and **Group mail from this contact** apply on this computer only and do not move messages in webmail or other apps.
:::

## One-click verification codes

When a sign-in or verification code arrives, you don't need to open the email and highlight it:

- **In the message list**: a **Copy code** button appears right on the row without marking the email as read.
- **At the top of the message**: opening the email shows a compact bar with the code and a copy button.
- **From system notifications**: with notification contents enabled, macOS and Windows let you click **Copy code** right on the notification (while MailX runs in the background).
- **Auto-clean after copying**: under **Settings → Mail → Enhancements**, turn on **Move email to Trash after copying a code** to tidy up automatically (includes a 5-second undo and never permanently deletes).

## Search, images and attachments

- **Search**: press `/` to search subjects, senders, previews, downloaded message bodies and attachment names on this computer. Unopened older bodies and text inside attachments are not searched.
- **Remote images**: to protect your privacy, images hosted on external websites ask before loading by default.
- **Attachments**: click any attachment in a message to save it to your computer.
